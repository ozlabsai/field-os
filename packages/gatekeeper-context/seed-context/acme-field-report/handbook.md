How the fictional Northwind field team classifies and escalates a visit.

# Field visit handbook

Fictional sample content, written to give an agent something with rules in it rather than only
numbers.

## Outcomes

- **Resolved** — the fault was fixed on site and the customer signed off.
- **Escalated** — the fault needs a specialist or a part the engineer did not carry.
- **Pending parts** — diagnosed, but blocked on a delivery.

## Escalation rule

Any visit running longer than **eight hours** is escalated automatically, whatever the diagnosis.
The intent is that a long visit gets a second pair of eyes, not that the engineer failed.

## Reporting

The team reviews repeat visits per site each quarter. A site visited three or more times in a
quarter is flagged for a standing fix rather than another callout.
